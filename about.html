The House of Jacketvoraix

Artisan Lineage

A Commitment to Protection

Jacketvoraix was established with a singular, unwavering mission: to rescue the fading art of true shear-cutting and introduce it to luxury technical outerwear. In a world increasingly saturated by thin nylon layers and synthetic glue seams, we dedicate our studio to Gore waterproofing membranes and full-grain calf leathers. We believe a jacket is not simply a shell; it is a structural representation of personal security, warmth, and tailored architecture.

Each textile roll is sourced individually from certified pasture cooperatives in Yorkshire. Once received at our New York lab, the membranes are inspected, pressure-tested, and cut entirely by hand. Hand-taping internal seams prevents water seepage without adding bulk weight, allowing the jacket to drape naturally. It is this minute attention to microscopic details that allows our products to sustain a Lifetime Hardware Guarantee.

Our Three Outerwear Pillars

01. Hand Taped Seams

We seal every internal seam loop-by-loop using custom polyurethane tapes, avoiding mass machine-gluing setups completely.

02. Lifetime Servicing

Traditional technical coats are made of repairable parts. If a zipper slider breaks, our cutters can mend it individually.

03. Bespoke Metallurgy

We mill our zipper pulls, snap buttons, and collar hardware in solid brass, hand-polishing and gunmetal-coating to prevent corrosion.

The Atelier Future

As Jacketvoraix looks to the upcoming decade, we remain anchored in our slow-craft principles. We maintain a small studio of certified tailors, limiting our global output to a few hundred curated outerwear pieces per year. This exclusivity ensures that every jacket shipped is personally inspected and pressure-tested. Jacketvoraix is a lifetime commitment to protection, urban aesthetics, and tailoring preservation.
